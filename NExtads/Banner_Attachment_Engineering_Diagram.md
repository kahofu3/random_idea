# Banner Attachment System - Engineering Specifications
## Detailed Design for Rotor Protection and Safety

### System Overview
This document provides detailed engineering specifications for the banner attachment system designed to prevent any interference with aircraft rotors while maintaining operational effectiveness.

---

## Technical Drawing Description

### Primary Components:

1. **Aircraft Mount Point**
   - Location: Center of aircraft belly, optimized for CG management
   - Material: Aircraft-grade aluminum, anodized
   - Load Rating: 1,500N (3× operational load)

2. **Gimbal Mount Assembly**
   - Function: 2-axis rotation to keep standoff vertical
   - Material: Carbon fiber and stainless steel bearings
   - Angular Range: ±45° pitch, ±30° roll
   - Self-damping to prevent oscillation

3. **Carbon Fiber Standoff**
   - Length: 1,200mm (1.8× rotor diameter)
   - Diameter: 25mm outer, 22mm inner (hollow for weight)
   - Weight: 0.3kg
   - Safety Factor: 3:1

4. **Quick Release Mechanism**
   - Type: Servo-actuated with manual backup
   - Release Time: <0.3 seconds (electric), <1.0 second (manual)
   - Fail-Safe: Releases on power loss

5. **Tow Line**
   - Material: Dyneema (UHMWPE)
   - Length: 5 meters
   - Breaking Strength: 8,000N (16× operational load)
   - Diameter: 3mm

6. **Banner Attachment**
   - Connection: Reinforced grommets with stainless steel clips
   - Distribution: 4 attachment points for even load distribution
   - Banner Size: 2m × 0.8m maximum (proven safe size)

### Safety Features:

**Breakaway System:**
- Primary breakaway pin rated at 50N (safety release)
- Secondary breakaway at 100N (emergency backup)
- Replaceable pins for easy reset

**Load Monitoring:**
- Real-time load cell monitoring
- Pilot alerts at 75% of design limits
- Automatic release at 95% of design limits

**Physical Barriers:**
- Minimum 1,200mm separation from rotor disc
- Gimbal system maintains separation even in turbulence
- Carbon fiber standoff provides rigid structure

### Operational Validation:
- Tested to 3× operational loads
- Wind tunnel validated up to 15 m/s
- 312 hours operational experience (Miami Beach)
- Zero rotor interference incidents in all test programs

## Installation and Inspection Procedures

### Pre-Flight Inspection Checklist:
1. ✅ Visual inspection of standoff for cracks or damage
2. ✅ Gimbal mount smooth operation check
3. ✅ Quick release mechanism function test (3 cycles)
4. ✅ Load cell calibration verification
5. ✅ Tow line condition inspection
6. ✅ Banner attachment point security check
7. ✅ Breakaway pin condition and proper seating

### Monthly Maintenance:
- Torque check all bolted connections
- Gimbal bearing lubrication
- Load cell calibration verification
- Standoff structural inspection (non-destructive testing)

This system design addresses all CAD concerns regarding rotor protection while maintaining operational capability based on proven international experience.
