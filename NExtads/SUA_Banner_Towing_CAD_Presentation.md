## Slide 3: Aerodynamic Analysis - Center of Gravity
### Maintaining Aircraft Stability and Control

**Center of Gravity Calculations:**
```
CG_new = (W_aircraft × CG_aircraft + W_banner × CG_banner) / (W_aircraft + W_banner)
```

**Design Parameters:**
- **Maximum CG Shift:** ±5% of Mean Aerodynamic Chord
- **Static Stability Margin:** Minimum 15% maintained
- **Lateral Balance:** Maximum 2% offset from centerline

**Example Configuration:**
- Aircraft Weight: 25 kg
- Banner Weight: 3.75 kg (15% limit)
- **CG Shift: 2-4% MAC** ✅ *Within Safe Limits*

**Verification Methods:**
- Ground balance tests
- Flight test validation
- Real-time CG monitoring
